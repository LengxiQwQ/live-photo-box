# P5-R2 — 普通图像可靠性

## Scope

R2 makes the R1 image execution truth evidence-based for ordinary, non-GainMap JPEG and HEIC only. It covers same-container passthrough, the existing Native JPEG lossless transform primitive, and JPEG↔HEIC lossy reencode. It records and validates the actual outcome for EXIF metadata, orientation, and ICC/color-profile carriage.

## Boundaries

- HDR, Ultra HDR, GainMap and auxiliary-image semantics remain R3; an image with those facts must remain fail-closed on an R2-only route.
- Color conversion and high-bit-depth policy remain R4. R2 may prove byte-preserved ICC carriage or report it absent/not representable; it must not call a profile relabel or a decode/reencode a color transform.
- Video, target-writer behavior, public Native ABI, backend selection and protocol authority are unchanged.
- A converter never mutates the source and never publishes an output after failed post-validation or failed cleanup.

## Acceptance

1. Every successful ordinary image route emits component outcomes based on a before/after preservation observation, not on `PixelReencoded` or decoder success alone.
2. Same-container passthrough is proven byte-identical and reports the applicable observed EXIF/orientation/ICC components as `Preserved`.
3. The existing JPEG lossless-transform route is selected explicitly, validates its output container and preservation observations, reports `LosslessTransform`, and does not claim preservation when a required observed component differs or cannot be parsed.
4. JPEG↔HEIC reencode has an explicit representability matrix for EXIF, orientation and ICC. It reports each actual component outcome; `Strict` rejects an unproven or non-representable required component before publish, while BestEffort may publish only with an explicitly non-preserved aggregate.
5. GainMap/auxiliary-containing inputs remain R3-owned and fail closed on R2 routes, with no output artifact.
6. Targeted unit, real-sample, and independent-reader/metadata evidence cover the routes above. Source hashes remain unchanged; Core/CLI build and unchanged Native ABI tests pass.

## Evidence

- Use the immutable `designs/各个机型测试/` corpus through the deterministic `.ai-tmp/cache/samples/` and `.ai-tmp/workspace/` locations.
- Native preservation observations provide project-owned structural facts; independent image/metadata readers validate published JPEG/HEIC outputs and do not replace structural authority.
- R2 may reuse the existing Native JPEG lossless transform and preservation-observation interfaces, but must not expand their ABI or turn external tools into production dependencies.
